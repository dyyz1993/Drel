## App Store 推广文本

### 简体中文

用声音开启互动：发声让角色跳跃，轻吹让蒲公英飞散。麦克风使用前须经你同意，授权可随时管理。

### English

Bring web experiences to life with sound: speak to jump and blow to scatter dandelion seeds. Microphone access is yours to manage.

## 版本更新说明

### 简体中文

- 新增声音互动能力，授权后可通过实时音量控制网页中的互动内容。
- 体验声音跳跳和吹蒲公英，麦克风授权可在权限中心管理。

### English

- Added sound-powered interactions for web apps after microphone permission is granted.
- Try Voice Jump and Dandelion, and manage microphone access in the permission center.
