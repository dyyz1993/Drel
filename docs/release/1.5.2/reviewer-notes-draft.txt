Drel 1.5.2 (Build 138) — App Review notes

No account, login, subscription or credentials are required.

Drel is a push-notification utility and a host for trusted signed HTML apps. Build 138 adds a user-authorized microphone sound-level action. Audio remains on device: the host supplies the page with a normalized sound level and does not expose raw audio or recording files through this action. It is not speech recognition or translation.

To review the feature:
1. Launch Drel, then on the home screen tap Manage next to My Apps and tap Browse All Apps.
2. Open Voice Jump or Dandelion from the signed app list. Opening the app alone does not request microphone access.
3. Tap the microphone start button. Drel asks for permission; cancel to confirm that the game does not start. Tap again and allow access; if prompted, allow the iOS microphone permission as well.
4. Make sound to control Voice Jump, or blow gently to scatter dandelion seeds. Stop closes microphone monitoring.
5. From the edge control, open More > Permissions and Capabilities to review or revoke the app's Drel permission. Leaving the page, backgrounding the app or revoking permission stops monitoring.

The demos are hosted at https://drel.app/sound/voice-jump/ and https://drel.app/sound/voice-dandelion/ and are available through the signed app catalog. The native capability is limited to a signed manifest, allowed origin, user tap and consent. Other web pages do not gain this capability merely by opening.

Drel retains its existing controlled actions for haptics, vibration, sharing, fullscreen, clipboard, location, add-to-home-screen and motion sensors. Clipboard, location, motion and microphone grants are user controlled.
