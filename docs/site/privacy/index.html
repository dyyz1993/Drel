Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Drel handles information in the iOS app, the official push service, and this website.

Plain-language summary

Drel is built to route your notifications and open HTTPS pages you choose from a notification. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising. The official delivery path is not an end-to-end encrypted secret channel: do not put passwords, recovery codes, financial data, private content, or authentication tokens in notification text, routes, or URLs.

Information handled by the app

Drel stores app settings, notification preferences, and notifications you choose to open on your device. Some delivery state is shared with Drel's notification extension through an iOS App Group.

Optional app permissions

Drel can give a trusted HTML app that you added to Drel access to two kinds of device data, and only after you approve each one separately. Clipboard access lets an app read or write the clipboard after you grant it. Location access lets an app request your position after you grant it, and iOS shows its own location prompt as well.

These permissions are listed in Drel's permission center, where you can see which app holds which permission and revoke it at any time. Revoking a permission takes effect immediately. Drel does not read the clipboard or the location in the background, and it does not keep the values that an app receives.

Drel does not access the camera, photo library, Bluetooth, microphone, contacts, motion data, or your files. The remaining native actions in Drel, such as haptic feedback, vibration, the system share sheet, and fullscreen, do not read personal data and do not create a stored permission.

Push delivery information

When you enable notifications, Drel receives an Apple Push Notification service device token and registers it with the official Drel push service. The service processes the token, a per-installation push address, notification content, delivery options, and destination route in order to deliver the requested notification.

Apple also processes push delivery under its own terms and privacy policies.

Open-source service

The WebBridgeKit server is open source. The Drel iOS app can use the official Drel push service, and it can also import a gateway configuration that you supply yourself, by QR code or by pasted text. An imported gateway is validated before it is used, and switching gateways does not carry over previous permission grants to a different app.

External web content

HTTPS pages opened inside Drel can communicate with their own servers and may have independent privacy policies. Only apps that you explicitly added to Drel, and that come from a gateway you imported, can call Drel's native actions. Ordinary web pages and offline packages receive no native access. Opening a page does not authorize a sensitive native action. You may open only pages that you own, operate, or are authorized to access, as described in the Terms of Use.

Website data

This website is delivered through Cloudflare, which may process standard network information such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, and security events to provide delivery, abuse prevention, and performance services. This site enables Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate, privacy-first traffic and performance measurements. It does not use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral analytics.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you remove it in Drel or uninstall the app. Push-service registration records are kept only as needed to provide push delivery and operate the service. Contact us for deletion requests concerning the official service.

Security

Drel uses HTTPS for its official service. No system can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid putting credentials or highly sensitive information in push content.

Children

Drel is a developer utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as Drel evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email privacy@drel.app. For product help, visit Drel Support.
